# AI Business Operations Toolkit  
A clean, modern, and genuinely helpful collection of AI prompts and branding tools designed to make your business run smoother, look more professional, and feel more put‑together.

Whether you're onboarding a new hire, writing SOPs, or trying to sound polished in emails and calls, this toolkit gives you the structure and clarity you need — without the overwhelm.

---

## 📁 What’s Inside

### 🧩 AI Prompts  
Clear, practical, and ready to use.

**SOP Creation Prompts**  
Create consistent, easy‑to‑follow SOPs for operations, onboarding, daily workflows, and internal processes.

**New‑Hire Onboarding Prompts**  
Welcome emails, training outlines, 30/60/90 plans, and role expectations that help new team members feel supported from day one.

**Professional Communication Prompts**  
Polished call scripts, email rewrites, tone improvements, and communication templates that help you show up professionally every time.

---

## 🎨 Branding Elements  
Tools to help your business look as good as it runs.

**Brand Voice Guide**  
Define how your business sounds — warm, confident, modern, or a mix that feels uniquely yours.

**Color Palettes**  
Curated HEX palettes that make your brand feel cohesive and intentional.

**Social Media Templates**  
Caption starters, carousel outlines, and content ideas that help you stay consistent without spending hours brainstorming.
