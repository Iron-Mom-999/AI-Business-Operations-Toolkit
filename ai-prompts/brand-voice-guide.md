# Brand Voice Guide  
Define how your business sounds across emails, social media, and customer interactions.

---

## 🎤 Voice Pillars
Choose 3–5 pillars that describe your brand’s personality.

Examples:
- Warm  
- Confident  
- Helpful  
- Modern  
- Friendly  
- Clear  

---

## 🎯 Tone Guidelines
Explain how your brand should sound in different situations.

Example:
- **Everyday tone:** friendly, conversational, approachable  
- **Instructional tone:** clear, structured, supportive  
- **Professional tone:** confident, respectful, concise  

---

## ✍️ Writing Style
- Use plain language  
- Keep sentences short and clear  
- Avoid jargon unless necessary  
- Lead with clarity, not fluff  

---

## 📝 Do & Don’t Examples

**Do:**  
“Here’s what happens next and how we’ll support you.”

**Don’t:**  
“We strive to provide exceptional customer service at all times.”

---

## 🔄 Rewrite Examples
**Before:**  
“Please advise how you would like to proceed.”

**After:**  
“Let me know how you’d like to move forward, and I’ll take care of the next steps.”

---

## 📌 Quick Checklist
- Is it clear?  
- Is it friendly?  
- Does it sound like us?  
- Would a customer understand it easily?
