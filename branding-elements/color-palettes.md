# Color Palettes  
Curated HEX palettes to help your brand look cohesive and intentional.

---

## 🌿 Soft & Modern
- #F7F7F7 (Light Gray)  
- #2E2E2E (Charcoal)  
- #6C63FF (Indigo Accent)  
- #FFB6A6 (Peach Highlight)  

---

## ☀️ Warm & Friendly
- #FFF4E6 (Cream)  
- #FF8A5B (Coral)  
- #2D2D2D (Deep Gray)  
- #FFD36E (Warm Yellow)  

---

## 🧊 Clean & Minimal
- #FFFFFF (White)  
- #E5E5E5 (Soft Gray)  
- #1A1A1A (Black)  
- #4A90E2 (Calm Blue)  

---

## 🎨 How to Use These Palettes
- Choose 1 primary color  
- Choose 1–2 accent colors  
- Use neutrals for backgrounds and text  
- Keep it simple and consistent
