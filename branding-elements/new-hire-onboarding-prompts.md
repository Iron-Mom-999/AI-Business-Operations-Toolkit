# New‑Hire Onboarding Prompts  
Help new team members feel supported, confident, and set up for success from day one.

---

## 🧩 1. Create a Full Onboarding Plan
**Prompt:**  
“Create a 2‑week onboarding plan for a new [role]. Include daily tasks, training modules, introductions, tools to set up, and checkpoints with their manager.”

---

## 🧩 2. Write a Welcome Email
**Prompt:**  
“Write a warm, professional welcome email for a new hire joining as a [role]. Include what to expect on day one, who they’ll meet, and how to prepare.”

---

## 🧩 3. Create a 30/60/90 Day Plan
**Prompt:**  
“Create a 30/60/90 day plan for a new [role]. Include goals, skills to learn, performance expectations, and milestones.”

---

## 🧩 4. Create a Training Outline
**Prompt:**  
“Create a training outline for a new [role]. Include tools they need to learn, processes to understand, and checkpoints for mastery.”

---

## 🧩 5. Create a Role Expectations Document
**Prompt:**  
“Write a clear, friendly role expectations document for a new [role]. Include responsibilities, communication expectations, KPIs, and what success looks like.”

---

## 🧩 6. Create a First‑Day Checklist
**Prompt:**  
“Create a first‑day checklist for a new hire. Include system setup, introductions, training, and a simple task to help them feel productive.”

---

## 🧩 7. Create a Manager’s Onboarding Guide
**Prompt:**  
“Create a manager’s onboarding guide outlining how to support a new hire during their first week, including check‑ins, feedback, and expectations.”
