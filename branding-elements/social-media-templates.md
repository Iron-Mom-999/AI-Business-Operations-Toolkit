# Social Media Templates  
Simple, modern templates to help you stay consistent without spending hours brainstorming.

---

## 📝 Caption Starters
- “Here’s something I wish I knew when I started…”  
- “A quick tip that will save you time today…”  
- “If you’re building a small business, try this…”  
- “Let’s make your workflow a little easier…”  

---

## 📚 Carousel Outline
**Slide 1:** Big, clear headline  
**Slide 2:** Problem  
**Slide 3:** Why it matters  
**Slide 4:** Steps or tips  
**Slide 5:** Example  
**Slide 6:** Call to action  

---

## 🎥 Reels / TikTok Script
**Hook:** “If you run a small business, this will help.”  
**Middle:** Show the process, tip, or transformation  
**End:** “Save this for later and share it with someone who needs it.”  

---

## 💡 Content Ideas
- Behind‑the‑scenes workflow  
- Before/after branding  
- SOP tips  
- Onboarding advice  
- Email rewrites  
- Productivity hacks
